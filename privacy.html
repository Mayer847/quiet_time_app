Privacy Policy — Quiet Time App

Last updated: 2026-01-23
Support contact: mayer.maximus@gmail.com

Quiet Time App is a simple daily Bible reading plan app. It shows the readings for the current day. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects (if any), how it is used, and your choices.

1) Data collection

Quiet Time App does not collect, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data. The app does not require account creation and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs in its current version.

2) Data stored on your device

The app may store basic app files needed for operation (for example, the embedded reading schedule file) on your device. It does not store personally identifiable information.

3) Data sharing

The app does not share data with third parties.

4) Permissions

The app is designed to run without requesting sensitive device permissions. If a future version requires additional permissions, this policy will be updated.

5) Children’s privacy

Quiet Time App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6) Data retention & deletion

Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no server-side retention. You can remove the app and any local app files at any time by uninstalling the app.

7) Changes to this Privacy Policy

If we add new features (for example, optional ads) that change data handling, we will update this Privacy Policy and the disclosures in Google Play (such as the Ads and Data Safety forms).

8) Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at mayer.maximus@gmail.com.

This page is provided for transparency and to support store listing requirements.
